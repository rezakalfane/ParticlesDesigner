Audio

new ParticleField("#particles", {
  look: "electric-bloom",
  audio: "microphone",
  audioDepth: 1.2, // optional: overrides the look's Audio depth
});
field.setAudio(document.querySelector("audio")); // keeps playing through the speakers
field.setAudio("manual");
field.setAudioLevels({ low: 0.8, mid: 0.3, high: 0.1, transient: 0.9 }); // your own analysis

Audio from another origin needs CORS (crossorigin="anonymous") or the analysis stays silent. Looks respond through their Audio depth (audioDepth overrides it); transients launch shockwaves (and the collapse cycle when the look enables it).
