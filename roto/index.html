ROTO-CONTROL setups

Play the Particles Designer from a ROTO-CONTROL: three setups on MIDI channels 9, 10 and 11 (channels 1–8 stay free for your other setups).

  • 9 PD PERFORM.json — LOOKS, SHAPES, GESTURES, VIEW
  • 10 PD DESIGN.json — PARTICLES, LIGHT, RIBBONS, ATTRACT
  • 11 PD MOTION.json — MOVE, SHOCK, CYCLE, AUDIO

Set up

  1. Import the three files into ROTO-SETUP and sync them to the device (setups 9–11).
  2. Connect the ROTO over USB and open the Designer in Chrome or Edge (Web MIDI). Allow MIDI access: it connects automatically (or press Connect ROTO).
  3. Every page: B4 Blackout, B5 Undo, B6 Redo, B7 Pause, B8 shows the page in the Designer.

The complete knob and button map is in docs/ROTO-CONTROL.md. The files are also on the CDN, e.g. https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/@absolumont/particles-designer@0.1/roto/9%20PD%20PERFORM.json.
